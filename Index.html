Structure of the Campaign

The Three Legs & Charities

Connecting the three disciplines of the sprint triathlon directly to the three bodies of Freemasonry and their dedicated philanthropic missions.

Symbolic Craft Masonry
LEG 1 • THE SWIM

Maine Masonic Charitable Foundation

MAC
Masons Assisting Communities
Youth & Community Support

The Swim represents the cornerstone and foundation. Just as water requires rhythm, balance, and trust, the Maine Masonic Charitable Foundation provides essential aid to local communities, youth programs, and brothers in need.

Shriners International
LEG 2 • THE BIKE

Shriners Hospitals for Children (Boston)

Shriners Boston Pediatric Care
Orthopedic & Burn Healing

The Bike represents power, momentum, and mobility. Enduring long miles on the Saris Fluid 2 trainer directly honors the children at Shriners Hospitals in Boston who are gaining mobility, healing, and restored confidence.

Scottish Rite (32°)
LEG 3 • THE RUN

Children’s Dyslexia Centers

Scottish Rite Dyslexia Centers
Unlocking Youth Learning Potential

The Run requires mental fortitude, grit, and step-by-step perseverance. Every mile on foot supports children with dyslexia as they overcome learning obstacles to unlock their fullest human potential.
